The Jump Smash: Timing, Power & Placement

The Kinetic Chain

Real power comes from the kinetic chain: legs load, hips rotate, shoulder follows, and the wrist snap arrives last, a split second before contact. This guide breaks the shot into the three phases coaches actually teach, with a drill for each.
