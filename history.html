History

Zephra started with a simple love for badminton.

Zephra was created in Singapore by Rian and Wen Rui from our shared love of badminton. What started as a simple interest in the sport grew into an idea for a community where players could learn, practise and improve together.

We wanted Zephra to be more than just information. Our goal is to bring useful techniques, experiences and resources together in one place for people who enjoy badminton.

Play together. Learn together. Improve together.
